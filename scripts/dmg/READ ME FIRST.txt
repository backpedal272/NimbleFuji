NIMBLE FUJI — READ ME FIRST
===========================

WARNING: USE AT YOUR OWN RISK
-----------------------------

Nimble Fuji talks to your camera over USB using a protocol that Fujifilm has
never documented. Everything it knows about that protocol was worked out by
reverse engineering, and some of it is guesswork.

Sending the camera commands it doesn't expect can change settings you didn't
mean to change, corrupt its custom settings or memory card, crash its
firmware, or leave it permanently unusable ("bricked"). Such damage may not be
covered by your warranty.

If you are not willing to accept these risks, do not install this app and do
not connect your camera to it.


INSTALL
-------

Drag "Nimble Fuji" onto the Applications folder in this window.

If macOS says the app can't be opened because it's from an unidentified
developer or can't be checked for malicious software:
  1. Open System Settings › Privacy & Security.
  2. Scroll down to the message about "Nimble Fuji" and click "Open Anyway".
  3. Confirm with your password or Touch ID.
You only need to do this once.


REQUIREMENTS
------------

- macOS 26 or later.
- A Fujifilm X-T5 (the only camera supported and tested, firmware 4.31).
- A USB-C data cable, connected directly to the Mac (not through a hub).


CAMERA SETUP
------------

1. On the camera: MENU › NETWORK/USB SETTING › CONNECTION MODE ›
   USB RAW CONV./BACKUP RESTORE.
2. Turn off AUTO POWER OFF while you work.
3. Connect the cable and switch the camera on.

Before its first slot write in a session, the app saves a full settings
backup of the camera to ~/Library/Application Support/Nimble Fuji/Backups.
Keep those backups.
